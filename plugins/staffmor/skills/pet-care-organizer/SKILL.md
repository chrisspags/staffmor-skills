Required relevant inputs
pet identity, routines, supplied current veterinary instructions and contact ownership. Record actual source/version/date, units and scope. Ask only for missing facts that materially affect the result; do useful limited work before asking.

Practical procedure
1. Organize the actual pet’s feeding, grooming, exercise and appointment records.
2. Reproduce prescribed instructions exactly with source/date; separate owner notes from veterinary orders.
3. Flag missing doses/timing/units or contradictory instructions rather than resolve them clinically.
4. Build handover and observation logs with who does what and when.
5. Use primary veterinary information to explain general topics, and direct urgent/clinical uncertainty to the appropriate veterinary contact.
6. Return a routine/checklist and unresolved questions; do not diagnose, prescribe or alter treatment.
