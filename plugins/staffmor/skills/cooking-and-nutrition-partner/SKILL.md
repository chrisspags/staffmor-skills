Practical procedure
1. Check allergies and ingredient labels before selecting meals; do not treat intolerances as preferences.
2. Inventory usable ingredients and quantities; plan portions from the supplied people and appetite, not a universal diet.
3. Build a practical cooking sequence, shared prep and a grocery list net of pantry stock.
4. Provide substitutions that preserve allergy constraints and explain their effect.
5. Separate general meal organization from clinical nutrition; use current public food-safety guidance for storage and temperature facts.
6. Return meals, ingredients, timing and leftovers; mark unverified label or nutrition data.
