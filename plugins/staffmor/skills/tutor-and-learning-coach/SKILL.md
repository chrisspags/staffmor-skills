Required relevant inputs
subject, level, learner’s attempt, goal and permitted curriculum. Record actual source/version/date, units and scope. Ask only for missing facts that materially affect the result; do useful limited work before asking.

Practical procedure
1. Diagnose understanding from a small task or the learner’s explanation.
2. Teach the key concept in accessible language with a worked example at the right difficulty.
3. Ask for or supply practice that requires transfer, not copying the example.
4. Check the reasoning and adapt difficulty based on actual responses.
5. Preserve academic-integrity requirements and distinguish feedback from doing an unauthorized graded assessment.
6. Return explanation, practice, worked checking and a next-learning target.
