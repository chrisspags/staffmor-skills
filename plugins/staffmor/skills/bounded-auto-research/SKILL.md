# Bounded experimental research

Recover the objective, accepted evaluator, incumbent, pending attempts and remaining budget from the existing research packet. Collect an existing attempt before launching another. Read the experiment record (included below). The name describes a research method, not permission for unattended workers, scheduling or unlimited compute.

1. Fix success and non-regression criteria before candidates. Recover prior decisions; ask only if a material metric, baseline or target is undefined. State units, direction and comparison formula. Passing correctness and meeting the improvement target are separate results.
2. Select the cheapest representative task that exposes the suspected mechanism. Reuse a compatible saved baseline; otherwise measure the unchanged artifact once. Confirm that the evaluator can detect the relevant failure before trusting a score.
3. Rank a short hypothesis batch by information gained per cost. For each, record the change, mechanism, predicted observation, falsifying result, fixed comparison parent and cheapest test.
4. Test one conceptual factor within the authorized finite unit. Retain candidate identity, raw output, resource use and failures. Missing output is unknown. Record keep, discard, inconclusive or invalid; do not weaken the evaluator or select a favorable baseline after results.
5. Test combinations only when the evidence and current brief justify them. Gains need not add. A focused winner stays provisional until the declared regression and confirmation checks pass, including uncertainty and failed attempts.
6. Deliver the best recoverable artifact and a parameter-effect map: measured effects, task-specific limits, harmful/null results, interactions, confidence and evidence. Return the completed finite unit to its coordinator. Any materially broader experiment, new worker or larger resource use follows the existing scope and machine admission rules.

Development cases are not held-out confirmation. Microbenchmark speed is diagnostic until the required end-to-end quality and cost checks pass. Research spend and optimized runtime/cost are separate quantities; unknown billing is unknown. At a stop boundary, account for pending jobs and leave an exact resume event or next proposed test. Do not schedule future work or claim optimality from a plateau.

# An interpretable experiment record

Reuse the existing research records; do not create another mutable queue.

**Contract:** outcome, evaluator/protocol revision, metric units, fixed baseline identity, improvement rule, correctness tolerances, representative inputs, resource/time limits, repair allowance and finite-unit done boundary.

**Attempt:** hypothesis, parent/candidate/source/model/prompt/data identity as relevant, prediction, command/configuration, seed, raw output, metrics, gate failures, actual resource use and disposition. Version a repaired candidate. Infrastructure failures are invalid or incomplete evidence, not a quality win or measured candidate regression.

**Effect map:** parameter or strategy, tested range, measured effect, affected task types, regressions, interactions, confidence, supporting attempts and uncertainty. Include null and harmful results. Mechanism explanations remain hypotheses unless separately tested.

**Handoff:** best verified artifact, active task, pending job/output location, remaining budget and next proposed test with its decision value. A wider coverage check requires a concrete unresolved claim, not an automatic benchmark expansion.

Example: a 60% baseline with a five-percentage-point target requires 65%; a 5% relative target requires 63%. Retain the owner's actual rule. Repeatedly tuned cases are development evidence. Untouched cases support generalization only when the evaluation protocol and separation were preserved.
