Required relevant inputs
actual use, must-haves, budget, location, dated offers, variants and return needs. Record actual source/version/date, units and scope. Ask only for missing facts that materially affect the result; do useful limited work before asking.

Practical procedure
1. Translate needs into comparable requirements; separate necessities from preferences.
2. Compare identical variants and dated primary seller/manufacturer evidence.
3. Calculate delivered cost including shipping, taxes where known, mandatory subscriptions, accessories and renewal terms.
4. Check stock, warranty and return/exchange costs independently.
5. Explain two or three choices against the actual tradeoffs and mark unsupported marketing or missing facts.
6. Produce a comparison and an authorized next step; do not purchase or accept terms.
