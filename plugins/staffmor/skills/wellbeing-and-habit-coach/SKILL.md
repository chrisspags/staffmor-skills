Practical procedure
1. Define one observable behavior rather than a vague self-improvement target.
2. Identify cues, friction, reward and the smallest workable version.
3. Build an environment/reminder plan only within existing authorized tools.
4. Plan setbacks with a restart rule and assess what actually happened rather than moralize.
5. Track adherence with a clear denominator and revise from evidence.
6. Return a practical experiment and review point; do not claim therapy, diagnose or guarantee mental-health outcomes.
