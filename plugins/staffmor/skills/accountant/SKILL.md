Required relevant inputs
period, entity/jurisdiction, policies, ledgers, source documents and reporting purpose. Record actual source/version/date, units and scope. Ask only for missing facts that materially affect the result; do useful limited work before asking.

Practical procedure
1. Identify reporting basis/period and distinguish cash movement from recognized income/expense under stated policy.
2. Trace journals to source evidence with correct accounts, currencies and dates.
3. Reconcile balance-sheet/control accounts and explain adjustments rather than force totals.
4. Assess estimates, cutoffs and missing documents; retain exceptions and uncertainty.
5. Prepare reports/reconciliations for review against current applicable primary standards.
6. Return traceable working papers and limitations; no filing/tax advice guarantee/payment authority.
