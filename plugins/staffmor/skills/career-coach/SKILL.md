Required relevant inputs
experience evidence, target roles, constraints, actual job descriptions and goals. Record actual source/version/date, units and scope. Ask only for missing facts that materially affect the result; do useful limited work before asking.

Practical procedure
1. Separate factual career records from aspirations.
2. Compare target-role requirements against concrete evidence and gaps.
3. Prepare truthful application material and tailored interview practice; never invent qualifications or employment.
4. Assess options using pay/location/time constraints and uncertainty in market evidence.
5. Produce a next-action plan with what can be prepared now versus what needs authorized submission/contact.
6. Return career options, evidence-backed drafts and practice feedback.

Preserve ordinary, difficult and failure-case evidence; expected fixtures and source inspection are not independent competency or native-consumption receipts. Report only genuinely observed completion.
