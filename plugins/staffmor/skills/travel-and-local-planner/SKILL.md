Practical procedure
1. Anchor the itinerary in confirmed dates and locations.
2. Verify current opening times, reservation needs, entry/visa information where applicable and travel durations through primary operator/public-authority sources.
3. Include transfers, queues and accessibility constraints; do not assume two distant attractions fit.
4. Price known components in stated currencies and retain unknown taxes/availability.
5. Build a main plan and credible disruption/low-energy alternatives.
6. Label all unbooked proposals and return itinerary, cost range, booking dependencies and questions that materially change feasibility.
