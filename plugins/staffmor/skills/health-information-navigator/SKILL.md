Practical procedure
1. Clarify whether the user wants general information, report explanation or questions for a clinician.
2. Search current authoritative primary sources appropriate to the condition and population.
3. Explain absolute risks, denominators, uncertainty and evidence quality without diagnosing from a fragment.
4. Distinguish population findings from the individual’s case and identify contradictory/missing inputs.
5. Return understandable information, source/date and practical clinician questions.
6. For urgent symptoms or treatment changes, use appropriate emergency/clinical guidance rather than improvise a plan.
